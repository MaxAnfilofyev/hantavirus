# Revised Outreach Note After Reviewing Other Virological Threads

Subject: Small GPC/background annotation complementing the ANDV MV Hondius analyses

Hi Dr. Ghafari,

I saw your Virological analyses on the MV Hondius Andes virus outbreak and reviewed the related Virological posts on the early multi-lab consensus comparison, deeper lineage timing, geographic/ecological origin, primer compatibility, and preliminary GPC selection analysis.

We did a narrow public-data follow-up that may complement those analyses rather than duplicate them. After translating Pathoplexus M ORFs and mapping them to Swiss full-length GPC numbering, the non-Canadian outbreak-associated M records in your May 17 metadata set map to `M/GPC 516I`, including both `PP_006WDKH.1` and `PP_006W6RC.2`. The two Canadian May 17 consensus records map as unresolved at that residue in our local pull.

This is not a within-outbreak discriminator among the non-Canadian records, and it does not contradict the finding that resolved coding-region differences within that set were synonymous. It is better viewed as a protein/background annotation: the outbreak-associated M/GPC state at Swiss-numbered 516 is uncommon in the broader mapped Pathoplexus M background and lies near a hydrophobic/membrane-proximal Gn region close to the intact `N524` glycosylation motif. The public `9P3Y` coordinates do not resolve this residue.

We also checked the posted preliminary GPC selection-analysis export. I did not see reference codon 516 itself listed as a significant selected site, and the thread reports no evidence for human-associated adaptation in ANDV GPC. So we are not making any claim about positive selection, transmissibility, entry, antigenicity, vaccine escape, clinical risk, or diagnostic escape.
