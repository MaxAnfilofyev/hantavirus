## Updated summary

After reconciliation with the Ghafari et al. ## Interpretation boundary

No evidence here establishes altered transmissibility, entry efficiency, antigenicity, vaccine escape, diagnostic escape, virulence, or public-health risk. Related Virological analyses are consistent with this conservative framing: they support near-identity of early outbreak S/M consensus sequences, congruent segment phylogenies without detected reassortment among sampled lineages, no elevated molecular clock rate on the outbreak stem branch, and no evidence for human-associated adaptation in preliminary GPC selection analysis. The appropriate handoff is: `M516I` is an outbreak/background GPC annotation in an unresolved membrane-proximal Gn region and is worth quick expert review, especially by ANDV GPC/entry or structural virology groups.
